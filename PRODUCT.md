# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

ASP.NET Core 10, SQL Server, React, TypeScript and Vite.

## Users

People at a Vietnamese small or mid-sized distributor who maintain the catalog, manage stock across warehouses, buy from suppliers, sell to customers and reconcile internal invoices and payments. Administrators manage roles and permissions.

## Product purpose

Keep operational records and their stock and financial effects traceable from the source document. The project also serves as a portfolio and a code-reading exercise for C# and SQL Server.

## Operating context

Daily work happens primarily at a desk. The interface is Vietnamese and the default currency is VND. The product covers internal operations and internal receivables and payables. Official electronic invoicing and statutory accounting are outside the brief.

## Product principles

Make document status and the next allowed action visible. Put quantities and amounts close to the records they summarize. Expose only authorized actions and explain blocked actions in Vietnamese.

Warehouse staff can compare current balances with posted movements and open reservations. Finance users can close a Vietnam business day and trace individual differences to their source document. Recorded sales costs and procurement price visibility follow the user's grants.

Buyers and sales staff can revise a draft and resubmit an order returned by a reviewer. The order records the reviewer, decision, reason and expected delivery or receipt date. Distinct approval grants govern self-approval and orders reaching the configured amount.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation and responsive layouts, including narrow screens. Identify status and validation errors with text.
