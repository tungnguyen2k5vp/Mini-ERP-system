# Requirements

A Vietnamese distributor manages master data, multiple warehouses, procurement, sales and internal finance in one application. Staff roles identify who can create, approve, post, cancel, report and administer records.

Stock on hand changes only on posting a stock document. Available stock equals on-hand minus active commitments and must remain nonnegative. Partial deliveries and receipts preserve order-line quantities. Posted movements and their cost snapshots remain available for reporting and reversals. Finance balances come from invoices and their adjustments and allocations.
